# Data Sources

This project uses publicly available humanitarian research and data sources.

## 1. International Organization for Migration (IOM)

**Source:** Displacement Tracking Matrix (DTM) – Sudan

**Website:** https://dtm.iom.int/sudan

**Data used:**
- Internal displacement datasets.
- Population return datasets.

**Purpose:** Analyzing displacement and return trends across Sudan over time.

## 2. Human Rights Watch (HRW)

**Website:** https://www.hrw.org/

**Data used:** Reports documenting conflict-related events and human rights violations in Sudan.

**Purpose:** Extracting structured information about conflict events and their geographical distribution.

## 3. Yale Humanitarian Research Lab (Yale HRL)

**Website:** https://medicine.yale.edu/lab/khoshnood/publications/

**Data used:** Research reports and satellite-based assessments documenting conflict impacts.

**Purpose:** Supporting the analysis of conflict events and their geographical context.

---

**Data Availability Note:** Original datasets are not redistributed in this repository. Users should access the original sources and review their respective terms of use.
